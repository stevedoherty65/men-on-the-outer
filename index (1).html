When a Mate Opens Up

Listen without judgement. You don't have to fix everything.

Listening tips

Send a Check-In Message

Not sure how to start? Copy a message and send it yourself.

See a message
Know the signs. Pulling away from mates, unusual anger, changes in sleep, increased drinking, or talking about hopelessness can be reasons to check in. Signs vary from person to person.
Try: “G'day mate, you haven't seemed yourself lately. Want to have a yarn?” Listen, take concerns seriously, and help them find further support if needed.
Message you can copy:

G'day mate, haven't heard from you for a while. Just checking how you're going. If you feel like a yarn, give me a call.
